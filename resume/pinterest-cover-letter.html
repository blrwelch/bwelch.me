To Whom It May Concern,

I've been a product designer for 17 years, spanning Fortune 500 e-commerce, enterprise platforms in regulated industries, and most recently the AI frontier. I feel I really shine at the intersection of design, technology, and user-thinking. The Staff Product Designer role on Pinterest's Design Innovation team is exactly what I've been preparing for.

Design systems are essential for a good, consistent user experience. I've built and maintained them for pharma clients, where a good design foundation and accessibility are critical. Recently, I migrated one of our clients' systems to Claude Design's Design Systems tool, adapting both the architecture and visuals to a different environment to simplify the design-to-dev workflow and enable faster, cleaner iteration.

Motion and visual design are central to how I think through a user's journey. How we use motion in interactive experiences has evolved over the decades. Good motion design is almost invisible yet measurable in the user's ability to move effortlessly through a good experience. Motion, to me, isn't aesthetic; it's a guide.

Pinterest's framing of AI as a collaborative tool matches how I work. I use AI tools every day, from brainstorming and rapid prototyping to integrating a whole design system. AI is a new way to do good design faster. For a platform investing in design innovation, it is essential. I'd love to join Pinterest's team and help contribute to what's next. Thank you for your consideration.
