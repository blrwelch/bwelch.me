Dear Pinterest Design Team,

I've spent 17 years designing products that have to work for real people at real scale. From Fortune 500 e-commerce to AI agent interfaces to enterprise platforms built for regulated industries, I've consistently operated at the intersection of craft, systems, and product thinking. The Staff Product Designer role on Pinterest's Design Innovation team is the kind of work I've been building toward.

Design systems are where a lot of my recent work lives. I've built and maintained them for clients in pharma, where consistency and accessibility requirements are unforgiving. More recently, I took one of those systems and migrated it into Claude Design's Design Systems tool, adapting component architecture and visual standards to fit an entirely different product environment. That kind of translation work requires knowing not just how to build a system, but how to evaluate one critically and evolve it without breaking what already works.

Motion and visual design are central to how I communicate ideas. I use Figma's advanced prototyping and Adobe After Effects to move from concept to something you can actually feel before a line of code gets written. At AutoZone, prototypes were how I got engineering and product aligned on a full site redesign that shipped with zero reported traffic loss. At C Spire, I owned UX, motion, and front-end development across the flagship wireless platform. I know how to design at a high level of craft and get it across the finish line.

Pinterest's framing of AI as a collaborative tool matches how I work. I use AI tools every day to compress exploration cycles, generate variations, and get to the real question faster. For a platform investing in design innovation, that's not a nice-to-have, it's the baseline.

My portfolio is at bwelch.me. I'd love to talk about the Design Innovation team's work and what I can bring to it.

Thank you for your time.
