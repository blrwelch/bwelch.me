A little more about me

Outside of work, my house is loud in the best way. My husband and I have been married for ten years, and somewhere along the way we ended up with five cats and two dogs. I wouldn't trade the chaos for anything.

I also collect Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles stuff, an obsession that started when I was a kid and never really left. My shelves have the proof.

If something in my house or garage breaks, my first instinct is to fix it myself before I call anyone. Cars, appliances, whatever it is, I'd rather figure out how it works than replace it. That same instinct is probably why I like 3D printing so much. I get to design something, watch it get built layer by layer, and hold the result. I also draw, mostly for myself, as a way to use a different part of my brain than the one I use all day at work.

None of this shows up on a resume, but it's most of what makes me who I am, and it probably explains a lot about how I approach my work too.
